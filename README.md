# NakliDawai Detector

## About The Project

NakliDawai Detector is a prototype web application developed to help identify fake or suspicious medicines using batch verification, OCR-based scanning, and community reporting.

Counterfeit medicines are becoming a serious issue in many regions and can directly affect public health and patient safety. Many fake medicines are sold with packaging that looks almost identical to original products, making them difficult for normal users to identify.

In some cases, fake medicines may contain:

- Incorrect ingredients
- Harmful chemicals
- Low-quality substances
- No active medicine at all

This can lead to:

- Treatment failure
- Serious health complications
- Increased medical risks
- Loss of trust in healthcare systems

The main purpose of this project is to create awareness and provide a digital solution where users can verify medicine details, scan medicine labels, and report suspicious medicines.

This project was developed as a college innovation prototype by Team Sublimation.

---

## Why This Project Is Needed

Fake medicines are a major problem in many places, especially where people do not have proper verification systems or awareness about counterfeit products.

Some common real-world problems include:

- Fake medicines sold using copied packaging
- Expired medicines relabeled and resold
- Duplicate or fake batch numbers
- Lack of awareness among customers
- Difficulty verifying medicines in rural areas
- Unsafe medicines sold without proper verification

This project aims to reduce these problems by using technology to make medicine verification simpler, faster, and more accessible.

---

## Real-World Problem Cases

### Case 1 — Fake Fever Medicines

In many areas, fake fever and pain relief medicines are sold using copied packaging of popular medicine brands. Patients purchase these medicines expecting proper treatment, but the medicine may contain low-quality or harmful substances.

### Case 2 — Expired Medicines Relabeled

Some illegal sellers change expiry labels and sell expired medicines again in the market. Consuming expired medicines can reduce treatment effectiveness and create serious health risks.

### Case 3 — Rural Area Medicine Verification

Many people in rural areas do not have access to proper medicine verification systems. A simple digital platform can help improve awareness and medicine safety.
